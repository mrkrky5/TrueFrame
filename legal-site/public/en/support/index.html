FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Your progress and library are stored only on your device.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Text is bundled in the app and reads offline. Cover images download on first open and stay cached. Source links need a connection.
Where is my data stored?
It is not sent to our servers. Deleting the app removes data on your device.
